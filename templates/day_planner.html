How the shared plan works

SunIQ reserves forecast household demand first, then evaluates appliance schedules together. Overlapping tasks share the remaining solar power; demand above that amount is counted as additional grid energy. Each task runs once without interruption and finishes by its deadline.

The bounded search tries different task orders and improves their start times. It suggests a low-grid-energy schedule; it does not guarantee the global optimum. Peak scheduled appliance demand: {{ '%.2f'|format(result.peak_kw) }} kW. This is not an electrical-capacity check.
