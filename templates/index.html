About the creator

Built by Arush

Arush is an 11th-grade student interested in learning electrical engineering and power electronics, especially in the clean-energy space. He believes the sun has far more to offer than we currently use. Visits to solar farms and time spent experimenting with circuit boards inspired him to pursue this journey and create SunIQ: a project that explores how real solar data and simple predictions can help small off-grid systems use energy more wisely.
