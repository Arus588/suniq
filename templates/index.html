Assumptions: constant 6 V, current increasing linearly to 5 mA at 100% sunlight. Power = voltage × current; energy = power × hours. This is an illustrative model, not your panel's rated output or measured energy. The energy estimate holds power constant; the trend control affects only the recommendation. Battery percentage is an input, not a charging forecast.

How recommendations work

Rules apply in order: battery below 20%, falling sunlight, or sunlight below 30% means conserve. Otherwise, sunlight at least 70% and battery at least 50% means run the load. Otherwise, battery below 90% means charge; remaining cases conserve. These demonstration thresholds have not been validated for a battery or load.

About the creator

Built by Arush

Arush is an 11th-grade student interested in learning electrical engineering and power electronics, especially in the clean-energy space. He believes the sun has far more to offer than we currently use. Visits to solar farms and time spent experimenting with circuit boards inspired him to pursue this journey and create SunIQ: a project that explores how real solar data and simple predictions can help small off-grid systems use energy more wisely.
