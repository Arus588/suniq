Privacy & data

Your solar workspace.

What SunIQ stores

Signing in with Google creates an account storing your Google account identifier, name, and email address. SunIQ also saves your solar provider choice, system name, optional site identifier, and imported readings.

What SunIQ accesses

Google sign-in requests only identity information. SunIQ does not read Gmail, collect your Google password, or automatically access your solar provider. Do not enter provider passwords or API keys.

Who can see your data

Your workspace is available to your signed-in account. The operator of the server can access stored data for administration. The public experiment uses separate project recordings, not your uploads.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on the server until you delete your SunIQ account. The account section deletes your account, solar setup, and readings from the app database. Google authorization can also be revoked in your Google account settings.

This is a student prototype. Imported data is not live monitoring or automatic equipment control.
